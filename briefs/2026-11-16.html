Monday, 16 November 2026

Nvidia’s founder is out, Serbia’s only refinery runs short of oil, and Iran fires drones at Bahrain.

01

Nvidia’s founder Jensen Huang steps down as its boss

Huang, who started Nvidia, the company whose chips power most artificial intelligence, left as chief executive and board member on Thursday, a day after the board met over reports of two affairs, one with a woman US officials suspect of spying for China. Finance chief Colette Kress is running the company while a permanent replacement is sought, and three committees of the US House of Representatives have called Huang in for questioning.

Sources: CNBC · NPR · Nvidia

02

Serbia’s only oil refinery runs short after a pipeline break in Croatia

Since construction machinery damaged the Croatian pipeline that carries crude oil to the Pančevo refinery on 6 November, deliveries have stopped and the refinery has cut how much fuel it makes. The government has let the refinery’s owner, NIS, draw on the country’s emergency oil stocks and has extended a cut in fuel tax, the charge built into the pump price, until 22 November to keep prices down.

Sources: Vreme · Serbia Energy · Serbia Energy

03

Iran says it fired drones at a US base in Bahrain

Iran’s Revolutionary Guards, the branch of its armed forces that answers directly to its supreme leader, said on Sunday they sent drones at a US radar on a base in Bahrain, their second such claim in three days. Bahrain, a small Gulf kingdom that hosts US forces, says it stopped the attack, and there is no independent confirmation of any damage.

Sources: Al Jazeera · A News

04

A big American rocket fails, losing 40 Amazon internet satellites

A Vulcan rocket from United Launch Alliance, a company owned by Boeing and Lockheed Martin, failed about ten minutes after lifting off from Florida this weekend, and the 40 satellites it carried for Amazon’s planned space internet service were lost. It was the company’s first complete failure since it was founded in 2006, and Amazon, which is trying to catch up with SpaceX’s Starlink, must now wait for an investigation before using Vulcan again.

Sources: SpaceNews · Spaceflight Now

05

Serbia drops out of football’s top Nations League tier

Serbia lost 3-1 at home to Germany on Friday, ending the match with nine players after two late red cards. With no points from five games, Serbia will drop to the second tier of the Nations League, the competition between Europe’s national teams, whatever happens against Greece tonight.

Sources: ESPN · TNT Sports
