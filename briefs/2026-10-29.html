A deadly train crash in Spain, no winner in Israel, and a terror alert in Belgrade.

01

At least 68 people die when one fast train hits another in Spain

A high-speed train run by Renfe drove into the back of a stopped train run by Iryo near Urda, south of Madrid, on Wednesday; the regional government says at least 68 people died and more than 100 were hurt, making it Europe’s deadliest rail crash since 2013. Spain’s transport minister said a computer fault had turned a signal to red, the sign to stop, and that the Renfe train was wrongly allowed to move on anyway, but investigators have not yet confirmed the cause.

Sources: Korea Times · IBTimes · Latin Times

02

Israel’s election leaves nobody with enough seats to govern

With nearly all votes from Tuesday’s election counted, Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu’s alliance has about 53 of parliament’s 120 seats and the camp of his rival, former army chief Gadi Eisenkot, about 48, while 61 are needed to form a government. Israel’s two Arab parties nearly doubled their share to about 19 seats, so whether Eisenkot will build a coalition, a governing partnership of several parties, with their support is now the central question.

Sources: Times of Israel · Ynet · Bloomberg

03

Serbia raises its terror alert after a knife attack at Israel’s embassy

Just after midnight on Wednesday a teenager armed with two knives attacked a police officer guarding the Israeli embassy in Belgrade, and the officer shot him dead, suffering a light cut to the face. Interior Minister Ivica Dačić called it terrorism, said the attacker had been watched by security services as a supporter of the Islamic State group, and raised the national threat level to its highest, which means more police at busy places.

Sources: European Western Balkans · AP via WRAL · Times of Israel

04

Qatar offers Iran a new plan to end its war with the US and Israel

Qatari go-betweens gave Tehran a draft deal setting out a week of step-by-step moves each side would take to build trust, with outside countries checking that both keep their word. Iranian officials have given mixed signals, some calling the terms unacceptable and others welcoming talks, and an answer is expected by the end of the week.

Sources: Reuters via US News · The National

05

Milwaukee wins baseball’s World Series for the first time

The Milwaukee Brewers beat the Tampa Bay Rays 4–1 on Wednesday night to take the championship series four games to one, the club’s first title in its 57-year history. Tampa Bay had stayed alive a night earlier by winning Game 4 in the 12th inning.

Sources: ESPN · NBC Sports
