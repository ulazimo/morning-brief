Monday, 2 November 2026

Tankers burn as Iran talks of reopening the Gulf, a record crypto theft, and Serbia remembers Novi Sad.

01

Iran says tankers hit mines in the Strait of Hormuz while it offers a plan to reopen it

Iran’s Revolutionary Guard says two oil tankers, one of them a giant supertanker, struck sea mines and caught fire on Sunday evening in the Strait of Hormuz, the narrow sea passage that carries about a fifth of the world’s oil and that Iran has kept shut since the war with the US and Israel began in February; no one outside Iran has confirmed the blasts. At the same time Iran’s foreign minister said that, after talks through Qatar and Pakistan, Tehran backs a plan in which the US eases its sanctions, its punishing limits on trade with Iran, on day one and Iran reopens the strait on day two, while President Trump says the war will end “very soon, one way or the other.”

Sources: Al Jazeera · Ynet · CBS News

02

The FBI blames North Korea for the biggest crypto theft ever

Hackers took about $6.3 billion in digital money from Binance, the world’s largest exchange for buying and selling cryptocurrency, around 29 October, and the FBI says a North Korean government hacking group called TraderTraitor did it. Binance says it is covering customers’ losses itself and on Sunday began letting people withdraw their money again, which led many to pull funds out at once.

Sources: Al Jazeera · ABC News · Business Standard

03

Serbia marks two years since the Novi Sad station roof collapse

Thousands gathered in Novi Sad on Sunday and stood silent for 16 minutes, one for each person killed when the concrete roof over the railway station entrance fell on 1 November 2024. The disaster started the student-led anti-corruption movement whose Student List won a majority in parliament a week ago, and which must now seat the new parliament and name a government.

Sources: AP via WRAL · RTÉ

04

Kimi Antonelli, 20, becomes the youngest Formula 1 champion

The Mercedes driver finished third at the Mexico City Grand Prix on Sunday, enough to win the 2026 world title at 20 years and 68 days, beating Sebastian Vettel’s record by about three years. Max Verstappen won the race ahead of Lewis Hamilton.

Sources: Formula 1 · Sky Sports
