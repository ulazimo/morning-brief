Thursday, 19 November 2026

Trump, Xi and Putin meet in China, Ukraine breaks the Crimea bridge, and Serbia’s parliament picks a speaker.

01

Trump, Xi and Putin hold their first three-way meeting in China

US President Donald Trump, Chinese leader Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin are due to meet together today in Shenzhen, China, on the edge of a summit of Asia-Pacific countries, the first time leaders of the three powers have sat down as a group. The wars in Ukraine and Iran, nuclear weapons, trade and artificial intelligence are expected to be discussed.

Sources: ABC News · Al Jazeera · CBS News

02

Ukraine brings down part of Russia’s bridge to Crimea

Ukrainian drones and missiles knocked the main road section of the Kerch Bridge, which links Russia to Crimea, the Ukrainian peninsula Russia seized in 2014, into the sea on Tuesday, according to images checked by Reuters. The railway beside it survived and trains are running on one track, while the Kremlin says the road will be rebuilt.

Sources: CNBC · Military.com · CNN

03

Serbia’s new parliament is set to pick its speaker today

The parliament elected after the student movement’s election win resumes at 10:00, with a vote expected this afternoon on Aleksandar Čupić, a university lecturer put forward by all 140 Student List members, for speaker. Whoever wins also becomes acting head of state until a new president is elected, and Aleksandar Vučić, now an opposition member of parliament, has said his side accepts the result.

Sources: N1 · Danas · Vreme

04

Anthropic’s share sale is the biggest on record

Anthropic, the company that makes the Claude artificial intelligence system, priced its first sale of shares to the public at $83 each, raising about $81 billion and valuing the company at about $1.5 trillion. Its shares begin trading on the Nasdaq stock exchange in New York today.

Sources: Globe and Mail / Reuters · AP via News4Jax

05

The US and Iran trade strikes again around the Gulf

The US struck Iran’s Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz, the narrow sea lane that normally carries about a fifth of the world’s oil, and Bahrain and Kuwait said they shot down weapons Iran fired back on Wednesday. Iran says it will keep the strait closed until the US lifts its naval blockade, while Trump says he does not need a deal.

Sources: CBS News · Al Jazeera
