Thursday, 5 November 2026

Democrats win Congress, the US and Iran trade fire near the Gulf, and Ebola deaths pass 5,000.

01

Democrats take control of both houses of the US Congress

In Tuesday’s midterm elections, held halfway through a president’s four-year term, Democrats won majorities in both the House of Representatives and the Senate, the two chambers that write America’s laws and approve its spending; the Associated Press called the House for them early on Wednesday. From January, President Trump will need Democratic votes to pass laws, and Democrat Hakeem Jeffries is in line to lead the House, after a campaign shaped by anger over the war with Iran and higher fuel prices.

Sources: Al Jazeera · CBS News · Forbes

02

US jets strike an Iranian island as peace talks stall

US officials say American planes hit Iranian military targets on Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz, the narrow sea passage for much of the world’s oil, after Iran’s Revolutionary Guard fired at a US fighter jet; Washington calls the strikes limited self-defence, not a return to full-scale war. Iran says the US answer to its plan for reopening the strait is “unconvincing,” and talks through Qatar have stalled.

Sources: CBS News · Al Jazeera · Gulf News

03

Ebola has now killed more than 5,300 people in Africa

The Africa Centres for Disease Control says the outbreak, almost entirely in the Democratic Republic of Congo with a handful of cases in other countries, has passed 10,400 cases and 5,300 deaths, making it the second-largest Ebola outbreak ever recorded. It is caused by the Bundibugyo strain, a type of Ebola for which there is no approved vaccine or treatment.

Sources: AFP via Medical Xpress · Vanguard

04

Serbia’s election winners are not yet naming a government

At a press conference in Belgrade on Wednesday, the Student List, which won a majority in parliament on 25 October, again declined to say who would be prime minister or ministers, saying the choice is still being made with the student movement. Instead it pressed the outgoing energy minister and the state power company to tell the public the truth about the state of Serbia’s electricity system.

Sources: Srpske vesti (citing N1) · NS Reporter · Vreme

05

Liverpool lose for the first time in this season’s Champions League

Fenerbahçe beat Liverpool 3–2 in Istanbul on Wednesday, while Real Madrid won 3–2 at AEK Athens with a Federico Valverde goal in stoppage time, the minutes added at the end for delays.

Sources: Yahoo Sports · Ahram Online
