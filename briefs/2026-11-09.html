Monday, 9 November 2026

Poland mourns a terror attack in Kraków, Serbia’s new parliament gets a date, and Nvidia’s boss faces questions.

01

A gunman kills six people in Kraków’s main square

On Saturday a young Polish man drove onto the busy old-town square in Kraków, Poland, and fired at random people, killing six and wounding about 15 before police shot and arrested him; the bodies of his parents and sister were later found at his home. Prime Minister Donald Tusk called it terrorism, meaning violence meant to spread fear for political ends, and the interior minister said the man appeared to be fascinated by far-right ideas.

Sources: NBC News · France 24 · CBS News

02

Serbia’s new parliament will meet for the first time on 17 November

The outgoing speaker, Ana Brnabić, set the first sitting for next Tuesday, and the Student List, which won 140 of the 250 seats in the 25 October election, answered with two words: “We are ready.” With that majority it can choose the speaker and approve a new government on its own, but it has still not said who its prime minister will be.

Sources: N1 · B92 · Washington Times (AP)

03

Nvidia opens an inquiry into its boss after reports of a link to a suspected Chinese spy

Jensen Huang, head of Nvidia, the company whose chips power most artificial intelligence, admitted an affair after US newspapers reported that American intelligence officials believe a woman close to him worked for Chinese spies; China denies it. Nvidia’s board has ordered an independent investigation but says it has no reason to think secrets were lost, while lawmakers from both US parties want him to answer questions.

Sources: Axios · CNBC · SF Standard

04

US–Iran talks stall, and the Gulf’s oil route stays shut

President Trump says new attacks on Iran are “highly unlikely” if talks make progress, but Secretary of State Marco Rubio says a deal is not close, and Iran is demanding that US sanctions, the penalties that block its trade, be lifted first. Meanwhile ships are still being attacked near the Strait of Hormuz, the narrow sea passage that normally carries much of the world’s oil.

Sources: Fox News · RFE/RL · CBS News

05

Manchester United and Aston Villa draw 4–4

United came back from two goals down on Sunday, while Liverpool won 3–1 at Crystal Palace and Chelsea lost 1–0 at Sunderland. Arsenal stay top of the Premier League, five points clear of Brighton.

Sources: ESPN · Sky Sports
