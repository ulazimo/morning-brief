Nvidia’s board weighs firing its founder, Russia and Ukraine agree a big prisoner swap, and Iran strikes an island in Oman.

01

Nvidia’s board meets to decide whether to remove Jensen Huang

On Wednesday evening the board of Nvidia, the company whose chips power most artificial intelligence, met to decide whether to push out Huang, who founded it and has run it for 33 years, after reports of two affairs, one with a woman US officials suspect of spying for China. No decision had been announced by this morning, and the company’s shares have lost about a fifth of their value since the scandal began.

Sources: Washington Post · CNBC · France 24 (Reuters)

02

Russia and Ukraine agree a large prisoner exchange, but no peace deal

After two days of talks in Abu Dhabi run by US envoys Jared Kushner and Steve Witkoff, the first direct talks between the two sides in nine months, both agreed in principle to swap a large number of captured soldiers, with exact numbers still to be set. The US envoys said they remain far apart on the big questions, such as who keeps which land, and are now travelling to meet Russia’s President Putin and Ukraine’s President Zelensky.

Sources: NPR · AP · Kyiv Independent

03

Iran strikes an Omani island that helps guide ships through the Gulf

Iran says it destroyed equipment on Oman’s Didamar island that helps ships find their way through the Strait of Hormuz, the narrow sea passage that normally carries much of the world’s oil; Oman confirmed a loud explosion there. The attack followed US strikes on an Iranian island nearby, though shipping sources say backup systems mean ships can still use the route.

Sources: Reuters · Marine Insight · The New Arab
