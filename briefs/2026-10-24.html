Saturday, 24 October 2026

Serbia votes tomorrow, France marches again, and a funeral in Jordan ends in gunfire.

01

Serbia chooses a new parliament on Sunday

Serbians vote tomorrow in an early election that pits Aleksandar Vučić, who stepped down as president last month to run for prime minister, against a list of candidates backed by the student protest movement that began after a station roof collapsed in Novi Sad in 2024 and killed 16 people. Today campaigning is banned by law, after a week in which police detained at least seven people they accuse of planning violence on election day and United Nations human rights experts warned that journalists and activists are under growing pressure.

Sources: Al Jazeera · BIRN · UN OHCHR

02

France puts 70,000 police on the streets for a second weekend of protests

More than 600 marches over the cost of living and police violence are planned across France today, a week after protests ended in looting and burned cars, and the government has allowed only marches organised by trade unions while cities such as Lyon, Marseille and Nice banned them outright. More than 100 people had been detained by early afternoon, and the Louvre and Musée d’Orsay in Paris are closed as a precaution.

Sources: France 24 · Euronews · Connexion

03

Gunmen kill seven at a church funeral in Jordan

Attackers got out of a car and opened fire on mourners at a Greek Orthodox church in a village in Karak, in southern Jordan, on Friday, killing seven people and wounding at least 34. King Abdullah called it terrorism, no group has said it was behind the attack, and police are still hunting the gunmen.

Sources: Washington Post · CBS News

04

Russia and Ukraine agree to meet again, and keep bombing each other

The Kremlin says Russian, Ukrainian and American officials will hold new talks in Abu Dhabi in the first half of November, and Ukraine’s president Volodymyr Zelensky confirmed the plan. The same night, a Russian attack on power stations cut electricity to more than half a million Ukrainians.

Sources: Al Jazeera · CNBC

05

Milwaukee wins the first game of baseball’s World Series

The Milwaukee Brewers beat the Tampa Bay Rays 3–2 on Friday night and lead the best-of-seven championship series one game to none. Game 2 is in Milwaukee tonight.

Sources: Bleacher Report · CBS Sports
